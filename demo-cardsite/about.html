The Realtor

A Local Guide With a
National Eye for Detail

Tess Ashbury grew up twenty minutes from where she now sells houses — which is either a small detail or the whole story, depending on who you ask. Eleven years ago she left a career in interior design to become a Realtor, convinced that helping a family choose the right house was really the same job: paying close attention to how people actually want to live.

Today she works almost exclusively across Williamson County — Franklin, Brentwood, and the neighborhoods just beyond — representing both the families moving in and the ones ready to move on. Her clients tend to describe the same thing: she listens before she talks, she's honest about a home's flaws as well as its charm, and she negotiates like the outcome actually matters to her. It does.

Outside of closings, Tess sits on the advisory board of a local youth mentorship program and can usually be found at her daughter's Saturday soccer games, phone turned face-down.

Beyond the Sale
“Closing day isn't the end of the relationship — most of my clients call me for advice for years after.”

Tess stays involved long after the keys change hands — from contractor recommendations to advice on when to eventually sell again. It's part of why the majority of her business now comes from repeat clients and referrals.
