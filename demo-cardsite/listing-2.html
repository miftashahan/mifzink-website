About This Home

A gray-shingle traditional on a tree-lined court, 42 Windemere Court pairs a classic covered front porch with a layout that's been quietly updated throughout. Hardwood floors run through the main living areas, the kitchen carries newer appliances and a bright breakfast nook, and a finished bonus room upstairs works equally well as a playroom, media room, or fifth bedroom. The fenced backyard backs to green space, with no rear neighbors in sight.
