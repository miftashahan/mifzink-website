About This Home

Set back on a quarter-acre lot with a stone-and-cedar-shake facade, 118 Ashgrove Lane was built in 2019 with an open-concept main level designed for both everyday life and hosting. The kitchen anchors the home with a waterfall-edge island and a walk-in pantry, opening directly onto a covered back porch with a wood-burning fireplace. The primary suite sits privately on the main level, while three additional bedrooms and a bonus living space occupy the second floor — ideal for a growing family or long-term guests.
