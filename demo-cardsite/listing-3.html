About This Home

Finished in 2021 with board-and-batten siding and stone accents, 27 Hollow Creek Way opens through a covered, cedar-beamed entry into a bright, open-concept main level. The kitchen's oversized walk-in pantry and adjacent mudroom were designed for a busy household, while a bonus room above the garage gives teenagers, guests, or a home gym its own separate footprint. A covered porch off the main living area looks out over a landscaped, lightly wooded lot.
