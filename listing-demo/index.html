Room by Room

The Property

A Home That Makes Room for Real Life

118 Ashgrove Lane was built in 2021 for a family that needed space without sacrificing warmth — and it shows in every room. The main level opens around a vaulted family room with a stone fireplace, flowing directly into a chef's kitchen anchored by a waterfall quartz island big enough for the whole family to gather around on a weeknight.

Upstairs, the primary suite is set apart from the other four bedrooms for genuine privacy, with a spa-style bath finished in marble-look porcelain and a walk-in closet built for two. A dedicated home office off the entry makes working from home feel intentional rather than improvised, and a mudroom with built-in cubbies keeps the chaos of daily life out of sight.

Outside, a covered back porch with its own fireplace looks over a fully landscaped, irrigated 0.6-acre lot — room enough for a trampoline, a garden, or just quiet mornings with coffee. A three-car garage and a paver driveway round out a property that was designed to be lived in, not just looked at.
