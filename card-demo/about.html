The Realtor

A Local Guide With a National Eye for Detail

Tess Ashbury grew up twenty minutes from where she now sells houses. Eleven years ago she left a career in interior design to become a Realtor, convinced that helping a family choose the right house was really the same job: paying close attention to how people actually want to live.

She works almost exclusively across Williamson County — Franklin, Brentwood, and the neighborhoods just beyond. Clients describe the same thing: she listens before she talks, she's honest about a home's flaws as well as its charm, and she negotiates like the outcome actually matters to her. It does.
